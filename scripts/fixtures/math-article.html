A Note on Continuum Equations

By Dana Reyes

The momentum equation can be written as a particular form of the Cauchy relation, whose general convective form is given below. It is the starting point for almost everything that follows, and it is worth reading slowly before moving on to the special cases.

By setting the Cauchy stress tensor to be the sum of a viscosity term (the deviatoric stress) and a pressure term, we arrive at the usual statement of the balance of momentum for a continuum.

Why the grouping matters[edit]

Writing the stress as a sum separates the part that resists deformation from the part that resists compression. That separation is what makes the incompressible case tractable, and it is why the two terms are almost always introduced together rather than one at a time.
