A Note on Small Rooms

By Dana Reyes

A small room forces a kind of honesty about what a space is for. There is no corner to put the thing you have not decided about, so the decision arrives sooner than it otherwise would, and usually more cheaply.

This is the argument for constraint that people find least convincing in advance and most obvious afterwards. Given room to defer, most of us defer. Given none, we choose, and the choosing turns out to have been the work all along.

The only section here

One heading is not a structure. It is a label on an essay that did not need dividing, and a table of contents built from it would be a list of one thing, which is a worse way of saying nothing than saying nothing.

The useful version of a contents rail begins at the point where a reader might genuinely lose their place. Below that, it is furniture that asks to be read before the thing it describes.
