## 2026-09-15

Report: "fails to generate all the graphs and images" on the same openai.com article,
after the chart work. Measured the live page against the reader figure by figure, which
named the gap immediately: charts were fine (12/12), the single `<img>` was fine, and
**all 6 video embeds were missing**.

Cause is not scoring this time, it is a whitelist: Readability's `_prepArticle` deletes
every iframe that does not match a hardcoded list of five video hosts. Any publisher
serving its own player loses every clip — see [[embed-preservation]].

Fixed with the same stand-in trick as [[chart-preservation]]; ads are still excluded by
host, and the shape travels as an `aspect-ratio` because the player was sized for the
site's column, not ours. Verified on the live page: 6 of 6 embeds, rendering and playing.

Also learned, before the diagnosis: Wikipedia keeps ~100% of its images because it wraps
every one in `<figure>`, and Readability's `isFigureChild` exempts figures from the
delete-heavy-image-blocks rules. That is the cheap general protection worth applying to
pages that do not use `<figure>` — not built yet, noted in [[chart-preservation]].

Extraction now runs ~5s on that page (chart switching dominates), so the popup button
says "Cleaning..." rather than looking frozen.

Smoke test 142 -> 147 checks.
